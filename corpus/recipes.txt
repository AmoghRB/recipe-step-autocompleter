## Masala Chai
1. Bring 1 cup of water to a boil in a saucepan.
2. Add the crushed ginger, 2 cardamom pods and a small piece of cinnamon.
3. Simmer for 2 minutes so the spices release their flavour.
4. Add 2 teaspoons of tea leaves and boil for 1 minute.
5. Pour in 1 cup of milk and bring it back to a boil.
6. Add sugar to taste and simmer for 2 more minutes.
7. Strain the tea into cups and serve hot.

## Jeera Rice
1. Rinse 1 cup of basmati rice until the water runs clear.
2. Soak the rice in water for 20 minutes, then drain.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon of ghee in a pan over medium heat.
4. Add 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds and let them splutter.
5. Add the drained rice and stir gently for 1 minute.
6. Pour in 2 cups of water and add salt to taste.
7. Bring to a boil, then cover and cook on low heat for 12 minutes.
8. Turn off the heat and let the rice rest for 5 minutes.
9. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve hot.

## Dal Tadka
Step 1: Rinse 1 cup of toor dal two or three times.
Step 2: Pressure cook the dal with 3 cups of water, turmeric and salt for 3 whistles.
Step 3: Let the pressure release naturally, then mash the dal with a spoon.
Step 4: Heat 2 tablespoons of ghee in a small pan over medium heat.
Step 5: Add the cumin seeds and mustard seeds and let them splutter.
Step 6: Add the chopped garlic and dried red chillies and fry until the garlic turns golden.
Step 7: Add the chopped onion and cook until soft.
Step 8: Add the chopped tomato, red chilli powder and a pinch of salt and cook until the tomato is soft.
Step 9: Pour the tempering over the dal and stir well.
Step 10: Simmer for 5 minutes, garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot with rice.

## Masala Omelette
- Crack 2 eggs into a bowl and whisk until frothy.
- Add the chopped onion, chopped tomato, chopped green chilli and chopped coriander.
- Season with salt and a pinch of pepper and whisk again.
- Heat 1 teaspoon of oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
- Pour the egg mixture into the pan and spread it evenly.
- Cook for 2 minutes until the bottom is set and golden.
- Flip the omelette carefully and cook for 1 more minute.
- Serve hot with buttered toast.

## Classic Pancakes
1. In a large bowl, whisk together 1 cup of flour, 1 tablespoon of sugar, 2 teaspoons of baking powder and a pinch of salt.
2. In another bowl, whisk 1 egg with 1 cup of milk and 2 tablespoons of melted butter.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
4. Do not overmix the batter; a few lumps are fine.
5. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
6. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and brush it with a little butter.
7. Pour ¼ cup of batter into the pan for each pancake.
8. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip the pancake.
9. Cook for 1 more minute until golden brown.
10. Serve warm with honey or maple syrup.

## Tomato Pasta
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Add the pasta and cook for 10–12 minutes until al dente.
3. Reserve ½ cup of the pasta water, then drain the pasta.
4. Meanwhile, heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
5. Add the chopped garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
6. Add the chopped tomatoes, salt and a pinch of chilli flakes.
7. Simmer the sauce for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
8. Add the drained pasta to the sauce and toss well.
9. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
10. Garnish with fresh basil and grated cheese and serve hot.

## Chocolate Chip Cookies
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, beat ½ cup of soft butter with ¾ cup of brown sugar until light and fluffy.
3. Add 1 egg and 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract and beat well.
4. In another bowl, mix 1 ½ cups of flour, ½ teaspoon of baking soda and a pinch of salt.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture and mix until a soft dough forms.
6. Fold in 1 cup of chocolate chips.
7. Scoop tablespoons of dough onto the baking tray, leaving space between each cookie.
8. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are golden brown.
9. Let the cookies cool on the tray for 5 minutes, then move them to a wire rack.

## Banana Bread
1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and grease a loaf tin with butter.
2. Mash 3 ripe bananas in a large bowl with a fork.
3. Stir in ⅓ cup of melted butter.
4. Add ¾ cup of sugar, 1 beaten egg and 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract and mix well.
5. Sprinkle 1 teaspoon of baking soda and a pinch of salt over the mixture.
6. Add 1 ½ cups of flour and fold until just combined.
7. Pour the batter into the loaf tin and smooth the top.
8. Bake for 50–60 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
9. Let the bread cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn it out onto a wire rack.

## Vegetable Fried Rice
1. Cook 1 cup of rice, spread it on a plate and let it cool completely.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a wok over high heat.
3. Add the chopped garlic and stir fry for 30 seconds.
4. Add the chopped carrots, beans and cabbage and stir fry for 3 minutes.
5. Add the chopped capsicum and spring onion whites and stir fry for 1 more minute.
6. Add the cooked rice and toss well on high heat.
7. Add 1 tablespoon of soy sauce, a pinch of pepper and salt to taste.
8. Toss for 2 minutes until the rice is hot and well coated.
9. Garnish with chopped spring onion greens and serve hot.

## Paneer Butter Masala
Step 1: Heat 1 tablespoon of butter in a pan over medium heat.
Step 2: Add the chopped onion, chopped tomatoes, cashews and ginger and cook until the tomatoes are soft.
Step 3: Let the mixture cool, then blend it into a smooth paste.
Step 4: Heat 2 tablespoons of butter in the same pan.
Step 5: Add the blended paste, red chilli powder and garam masala and cook for 5 minutes.
Step 6: Add ½ cup of water and salt to taste and bring to a simmer.
Step 7: Add the paneer cubes and simmer for 3 minutes.
Step 8: Stir in 2 tablespoons of cream and a pinch of crushed kasuri methi.
Step 9: Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot with naan or rice.

## Aloo Paratha
1. Boil 3 potatoes until soft, then peel and mash them.
2. Mix the mashed potatoes with chopped green chilli, chopped coriander, salt and garam masala.
3. Knead 2 cups of wheat flour with water and a pinch of salt into a soft dough.
4. Cover the dough and let it rest for 15 minutes.
5. Divide the dough into equal balls and roll each ball into a small circle.
6. Place a spoonful of the potato filling in the centre and seal the edges.
7. Roll the stuffed ball gently into a flat paratha.
8. Heat a tawa over medium heat and cook the paratha until brown spots appear on both sides.
9. Brush the paratha with ghee and cook until golden and crisp.
10. Serve hot with curd and pickle.

## Upma
1. Dry roast 1 cup of rava in a pan over low heat until fragrant, then set aside.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in the same pan over medium heat.
3. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter.
4. Add the urad dal, chana dal and curry leaves and fry until the dal turns golden.
5. Add the chopped onion, chopped green chilli and grated ginger and cook until the onion is soft.
6. Pour in 2 ½ cups of water and add salt to taste.
7. Bring the water to a boil.
8. Add the roasted rava slowly, stirring all the time so no lumps form.
9. Cover and cook on low heat for 3 minutes.
10. Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot.

## Poha
- Rinse 2 cups of thick poha in a strainer and let it drain for 5 minutes.
- Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
- Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter.
- Add the peanuts and fry until they are golden.
- Add the curry leaves, chopped green chilli and chopped onion and cook until the onion is soft.
- Add turmeric, salt and a pinch of sugar and stir well.
- Add the drained poha and mix gently so it does not break.
- Cover and cook on low heat for 2 minutes.
- Squeeze lemon juice over the poha, garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot.

## Lemon Rice
1. Cook 1 cup of rice and let it cool completely.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
3. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter.
4. Add the chana dal, urad dal and peanuts and fry until golden.
5. Add the curry leaves, dried red chillies and a pinch of asafoetida.
6. Add turmeric and stir for a few seconds.
7. Add the cooked rice and salt to taste and mix gently.
8. Turn off the heat and squeeze the juice of 1 lemon over the rice.
9. Mix well, garnish with chopped coriander and serve.

## Chicken Curry
Step 1: Marinate the chicken with curd, turmeric, red chilli powder and salt for 30 minutes.
Step 2: Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a heavy pan over medium heat.
Step 3: Add the bay leaf, cloves, cardamom and cinnamon and fry for a few seconds.
Step 4: Add the sliced onions and cook until golden brown.
Step 5: Add the ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute until the raw smell goes away.
Step 6: Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and the oil separates.
Step 7: Add the coriander powder, cumin powder and garam masala and cook for 2 minutes.
Step 8: Add the marinated chicken and cook on high heat for 5 minutes.
Step 9: Pour in 1 cup of water, cover and simmer for 20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
Step 10: Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot with rice or roti.

## Roast Vegetables
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Cut the potatoes, carrots, onions and capsicum into large even pieces.
3. Toss the vegetables with olive oil, salt, pepper and dried herbs.
4. Spread the vegetables in a single layer on a baking tray.
5. Roast for 30–35 minutes, turning them halfway through.
6. Roast until the vegetables are tender and golden at the edges.
7. Squeeze a little lemon juice over the vegetables and serve warm.

## Garlic Bread
1. Preheat the oven to 190°C.
2. Mix ¼ cup of soft butter with the chopped garlic, chopped parsley and a pinch of salt.
3. Cut the bread into thick slices without cutting all the way through.
4. Spread the garlic butter generously between the slices.
5. Wrap the bread in foil and bake for 10 minutes.
6. Open the foil and bake for 5 more minutes until the top is crisp and golden.
7. Serve warm.

## French Toast
1. Whisk 2 eggs with ½ cup of milk, 1 teaspoon of sugar and a pinch of cinnamon.
2. Dip each slice of bread into the egg mixture so both sides are coated.
3. Heat a little butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
4. Place the bread in the pan and cook for 2 minutes until golden brown.
5. Flip the bread and cook the other side for 2 more minutes.
6. Serve warm with honey and fresh fruit.

## Guacamole
- Cut the avocados in half, remove the seed and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
- Mash the avocado with a fork, leaving it a little chunky.
- Add the chopped onion, chopped tomato, chopped green chilli and chopped coriander.
- Squeeze in the juice of 1 lime and season with salt.
- Mix gently and taste for salt.
- Serve right away with chips.

## Mushroom Soup
1. Heat 2 tablespoons of butter in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onion and chopped garlic and cook until soft.
3. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook for 8 minutes until they release their water.
4. Sprinkle 1 tablespoon of flour over the mushrooms and stir for 1 minute.
5. Pour in 3 cups of vegetable stock slowly, stirring all the time.
6. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes.
7. Blend half of the soup until smooth and stir it back into the pot.
8. Stir in ½ cup of cream and season with salt and pepper.
9. Serve hot with toasted bread.

## Vegetable Biryani
Step 1: Rinse 1 ½ cups of basmati rice and soak it in water for 30 minutes.
Step 2: Boil the rice in plenty of salted water until it is 70 percent cooked, then drain.
Step 3: Heat 3 tablespoons of ghee in a heavy pot over medium heat.
Step 4: Add the bay leaf, cloves, cardamom and cinnamon and fry for a few seconds.
Step 5: Add the sliced onions and cook until golden brown.
Step 6: Add the ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
Step 7: Add the chopped vegetables, curd, biryani masala and salt and cook for 5 minutes.
Step 8: Spread the half cooked rice over the vegetables in an even layer.
Step 9: Sprinkle fried onions, chopped mint, chopped coriander and saffron milk over the rice.
Step 10: Cover tightly and cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
Step 11: Let the biryani rest for 5 minutes, then mix gently and serve hot with raita.

## Rava Kesari
1. Heat 2 tablespoons of ghee in a pan over low heat.
2. Fry the cashews and raisins until golden, then set them aside.
3. Add 1 cup of rava to the same pan and roast until fragrant.
4. Meanwhile, bring 2 cups of water to a boil with a pinch of saffron.
5. Pour the hot water into the rava slowly, stirring all the time so no lumps form.
6. Cook until the rava absorbs all the water.
7. Add 1 cup of sugar and stir until it melts.
8. Add 2 more tablespoons of ghee and a pinch of cardamom powder and stir well.
9. Garnish with the fried cashews and raisins and serve warm.

## Coconut Chutney
1. Add 1 cup of grated coconut, 2 green chillies, a small piece of ginger and salt to a blender.
2. Add 2 tablespoons of roasted chana dal and ¼ cup of water.
3. Blend into a smooth paste, adding more water if needed.
4. Pour the chutney into a bowl.
5. Heat 1 teaspoon of oil in a small pan over medium heat.
6. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter.
7. Add the curry leaves and a dried red chilli and fry for a few seconds.
8. Pour the tempering over the chutney and serve with idli or dosa.

## Chana Masala
1. Soak 1 cup of chickpeas in water overnight.
2. Pressure cook the chickpeas with salt and water for 5 whistles until soft.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
4. Add the cumin seeds and let them splutter.
5. Add the chopped onion and cook until golden brown.
6. Add the ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
7. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
8. Add the chana masala powder, red chilli powder and turmeric and cook for 2 minutes.
9. Add the cooked chickpeas along with 1 cup of their cooking water.
10. Simmer for 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas to thicken the gravy.
11. Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot with rice or bhature.

## Scrambled Eggs
- Crack 3 eggs into a bowl, add a pinch of salt and whisk well.
- Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in a non-stick pan over low heat.
- Pour in the eggs and let them sit for a few seconds.
- Stir gently with a spatula, pushing the eggs from the edges to the centre.
- Take the pan off the heat while the eggs are still a little soft.
- Season with pepper and serve hot on toast.

## Grilled Cheese Sandwich
1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
2. Place a slice of cheese between two slices of bread, buttered sides out.
3. Heat a pan over medium heat.
4. Place the sandwich in the pan and cook for 3 minutes until golden brown.
5. Flip the sandwich and cook for 2 more minutes until the cheese melts.
6. Cut the sandwich in half and serve hot.

## Greek Salad
1. Cut the cucumbers, tomatoes and capsicum into bite sized pieces.
2. Slice the red onion thinly.
3. Add all the vegetables to a large bowl.
4. Add the olives and the feta cheese.
5. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, dried oregano, salt and pepper in a small bowl.
6. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently.
7. Serve right away.

## Mango Lassi
1. Peel the mango and chop the flesh into pieces.
2. Add the chopped mango, 1 cup of curd, ½ cup of milk and 2 tablespoons of sugar to a blender.
3. Add a pinch of cardamom powder and a few ice cubes.
4. Blend until smooth and frothy.
5. Taste and add more sugar if needed.
6. Pour into glasses and serve chilled.

## Vanilla Sponge Cake
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a round cake tin with butter.
2. Line the base of the tin with parchment paper.
3. In a large bowl, beat 3 eggs with ¾ cup of sugar until pale and thick.
4. Add 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract and beat well.
5. Sift 1 cup of flour and 1 teaspoon of baking powder over the egg mixture.
6. Fold the flour in gently so the batter stays light.
7. Fold in ¼ cup of melted butter.
8. Pour the batter into the cake tin and tap it on the counter to remove air bubbles.
9. Bake for 25–30 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
10. Let the cake cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn it out onto a wire rack.

## Hakka Noodles
1. Boil the noodles in salted water for 3 minutes, then drain.
2. Rinse the noodles under cold water and toss them with a little oil so they do not stick.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a wok over high heat.
4. Add the chopped garlic and chopped ginger and stir fry for 30 seconds.
5. Add the sliced onion, sliced capsicum, shredded cabbage and sliced carrots and stir fry for 2 minutes.
6. Add the noodles and toss well on high heat.
7. Add 1 tablespoon of soy sauce, 1 teaspoon of vinegar, a pinch of pepper and salt to taste.
8. Toss for 2 minutes until the noodles are hot and well coated.
9. Garnish with chopped spring onion greens and serve hot.

## Pav Bhaji
Step 1: Boil the potatoes, cauliflower, peas and carrots until soft, then mash them.
Step 2: Heat 3 tablespoons of butter in a large pan over medium heat.
Step 3: Add the chopped onion and cook until soft.
Step 4: Add the ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
Step 5: Add the chopped capsicum and chopped tomatoes and cook until the tomatoes are soft.
Step 6: Add the pav bhaji masala, red chilli powder, turmeric and salt and cook for 2 minutes.
Step 7: Add the mashed vegetables and ½ cup of water and mash everything together.
Step 8: Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring often.
Step 9: Slit the pav and toast it on a hot tawa with butter until golden.
Step 10: Garnish the bhaji with chopped onion, chopped coriander and a squeeze of lemon and serve with the pav.

## Rasam
1. Boil the chopped tomatoes with 2 cups of water, tamarind pulp, turmeric and salt for 10 minutes.
2. Add the rasam powder and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Add ½ cup of cooked toor dal and 1 cup of water and bring it to a gentle boil.
4. Turn off the heat as soon as the rasam turns frothy.
5. Heat 1 tablespoon of ghee in a small pan over medium heat.
6. Add the mustard seeds, cumin seeds and a pinch of asafoetida and let them splutter.
7. Add the curry leaves and dried red chillies and fry for a few seconds.
8. Pour the tempering over the rasam, garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot with rice.

## Brownies
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a square baking tin with parchment paper.
2. Melt ½ cup of butter and 1 cup of chopped dark chocolate together over low heat.
3. Let the chocolate mixture cool for 5 minutes.
4. Whisk in 1 cup of sugar, then whisk in 2 eggs one at a time.
5. Add 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract and mix well.
6. Fold in ½ cup of flour, 2 tablespoons of cocoa powder and a pinch of salt.
7. Pour the batter into the tin and smooth the top.
8. Bake for 20–25 minutes until the top is set but the centre is still a little soft.
9. Let the brownies cool completely before cutting them into squares.

## Palak Paneer
1. Blanch the spinach leaves in boiling water for 2 minutes.
2. Plunge the spinach into ice cold water, then drain it.
3. Blend the spinach with the green chillies into a smooth puree.
4. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
5. Add the cumin seeds and let them splutter.
6. Add the chopped onion and cook until soft.
7. Add the ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
8. Add the chopped tomato and cook until soft.
9. Add the spinach puree, garam masala and salt and simmer for 5 minutes.
10. Add the paneer cubes and simmer for 3 minutes.
11. Stir in 2 tablespoons of cream and serve hot with roti.

## Oats Porridge
- Bring 1 cup of milk and ½ cup of water to a boil in a small pot.
- Add ½ cup of rolled oats and a pinch of salt.
- Cook on low heat for 5 minutes, stirring often.
- Stir in 1 teaspoon of honey and a pinch of cinnamon.
- Pour the porridge into a bowl.
- Top with sliced banana and chopped nuts and serve warm.

## Mashed Potatoes
1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into even pieces.
2. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 15–20 minutes until soft.
3. Drain the potatoes and let them steam dry for 2 minutes.
4. Mash the potatoes until smooth.
5. Stir in 2 tablespoons of butter and ½ cup of warm milk.
6. Season with salt and pepper and serve hot.

## Fish Fry
1. Wash the fish pieces and pat them dry with a kitchen towel.
2. Mix the red chilli powder, turmeric, ginger garlic paste, lemon juice and salt into a paste.
3. Coat the fish pieces with the paste and marinate for 20 minutes.
4. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a pan over medium heat.
5. Place the fish pieces in the pan in a single layer.
6. Fry for 4 minutes until the bottom is crisp and golden.
7. Flip the fish and fry the other side for 3 more minutes.
8. Serve hot with sliced onion and lemon wedges.

## Idli
1. Soak 2 cups of idli rice and ½ cup of urad dal separately for 6 hours.
2. Grind the urad dal into a smooth, fluffy batter, adding a little water at a time.
3. Grind the rice into a slightly coarse batter.
4. Mix both batters together with salt in a large bowl.
5. Cover the bowl and let the batter ferment overnight in a warm place.
6. Grease the idli plates with a little oil.
7. Pour the batter into the idli plates.
8. Steam the idlis for 10–12 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
9. Let the idlis rest for 2 minutes, then remove them from the plates.
10. Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Tomato Soup
1. Heat 1 tablespoon of butter in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add the chopped onion and chopped garlic and cook until soft.
3. Add the chopped tomatoes, salt and a pinch of sugar.
4. Pour in 2 cups of water and bring to a boil.
5. Simmer for 15 minutes until the tomatoes are very soft.
6. Let the soup cool a little, then blend it until smooth.
7. Strain the soup back into the pot.
8. Season with pepper and simmer for 2 more minutes.
9. Stir in a little cream and serve hot with croutons.

## Margherita Pizza
1. Mix 2 cups of flour, 1 teaspoon of yeast, 1 teaspoon of sugar and a pinch of salt in a large bowl.
2. Add ¾ cup of warm water and 1 tablespoon of olive oil and knead into a soft dough.
3. Cover the dough and let it rise in a warm place for 1 hour.
4. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
5. Punch the dough down and roll it into a thin circle.
6. Place the base on a baking tray and spread the pizza sauce over it.
7. Top with grated mozzarella cheese and fresh basil leaves.
8. Drizzle with a little olive oil.
9. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
10. Let the pizza cool for 2 minutes, then slice and serve.

## Sambar
1. Pressure cook 1 cup of toor dal with turmeric and water for 3 whistles, then mash it.
2. Boil the chopped drumsticks, carrots and onions in water with salt until tender.
3. Add the tamarind pulp and sambar powder and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Add the mashed dal and 1 cup of water and bring to a boil.
5. Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a small pan over medium heat.
7. Add the mustard seeds, a pinch of asafoetida and dried red chillies and let them splutter.
8. Add the curry leaves and fry for a few seconds.
9. Pour the tempering over the sambar, garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot with rice or idli.

## Dosa
1. Heat a tawa over medium heat and sprinkle a few drops of water on it.
2. When the water sizzles, wipe the tawa with a cloth dipped in oil.
3. Pour a ladle of dosa batter in the centre of the tawa.
4. Spread the batter in a circular motion to make a thin dosa.
5. Drizzle a little oil around the edges.
6. Cook until the bottom is crisp and golden brown.
7. Fold the dosa in half and serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Kheer
1. Rinse ¼ cup of rice and soak it for 20 minutes.
2. Bring 4 cups of milk to a boil in a heavy pot.
3. Add the drained rice and cook on low heat for 30 minutes, stirring often.
4. Cook until the rice is soft and the milk thickens.
5. Add ½ cup of sugar and stir until it melts.
6. Add a pinch of cardamom powder and a few strands of saffron.
7. Garnish with chopped nuts and serve warm or chilled.
